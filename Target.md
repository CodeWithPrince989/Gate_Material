2. Har topic ke liye yeh 5-step method use karo
1

Read — 15 minutes
Ek chhota topic padho, jaise variables, data types, printf() ya scanf(). Ek sitting mein poora chapter khatam karne ki koshish mat karo.


2

Understand — 10 minutes
Book band karke apne words mein explain karo: yeh concept kya hai, kaise kaam karta hai aur kahan use hota hai?


3

Predict — 10 minutes
Example code ka output pehle khud predict karo. Variables ki values ko paper par trace karo, phir actual output check karo.


4

Code — 20 minutes
Book ka example hide karke khud type karo. Uske baad numbers, conditions ya inputs change karke dekho.


5

Recall — 5 minutes
Bina book dekhe likho: aaj kya seekha, ek example, aur ek mistake jo tumne ki.



Ek 60-minute study session. Difficult topic ho toh ise do sessions mein divide kar sakte ho.




Theory book padhne ka 5-step method









Step 1: Topic ko samjho — 15 minutes
Poora chapter ek saath mat padho. Ek chhota subtopic choose karo, jaise OSI Model, Normalization ya Deadlock.
Pehle heading, diagram aur main idea samjho. Har sentence ratne ki zaroorat nahi.












Step 2: Book band karke explain karo — 10 minutes
Apne aap se poochho: Yeh kya hai? Kyun use hota hai? Kaise kaam karta hai?
Example: OSI model mein 7 layers kyun hain aur har layer ka kaam kya hai?












Step 3: Short notes banao — 10 minutes
Har topic ke liye ek compact summary, important diagram, formulas aur confusing points likho.
Book ko dobara copy mat karo.












Step 4: Questions solve karo — 20 minutes
GATE PYQs aur topic-wise questions attempt karo. Answer dekhne se pehle apna reasoning likho.












Step 5: Active recall aur revision — 5 minutes
Book band karke main concepts yaad karo. Jo bhool gaye, sirf wahi dobara dekho.
Agle din, 3 din baad aur 7 din baad revise karo.

---

Book, lectures aur PYQs ko kaise combine karna hai?
Tum GoClasses se GATE prepare kar rahe ho, toh yeh order follow karo:
1. Lecture dekho

Topic ka overview aur conceptual explanation lo.

2. Book se wahi topic padho

Lecture mein unclear points aur important details clarify karo. Pura chapter dobara padhne ki zaroorat nahi.

3. Book band karke recall karo

Definition, diagram, algorithm ya formula memory se reproduce karo.

4. Topic-wise PYQs solve karo

Galat questions ko mark karo aur unka reason note karo.

5. Spaced revision karo

1 day, 3 days aur 7 days ke interval par recall test lo.



---

3 din baad revision kaise karna hai?

Step 1 — Book band rakho (5 minutes)
Ek blank page par likho ki tumhe topic ke baare mein kya yaad hai.
Example: OSI ki 7 layers, unke functions aur important protocols.




Step 2 — Khud se questions poochho (5 minutes)
- Network layer aur Transport layer mein kya difference hai?
- Routing kis layer par hoti hai?
- TCP aur UDP ka role kya hai?




Step 3 — Ab book kholo (5–10 minutes)
Apne answers ko book se compare karo. Jo sahi tha usko tick karo; jo incomplete ya galat tha, usko mark karo.




Step 4 — Weak parts dobara padho (5–10 minutes)
Sirf galat ya bhule hue concepts padho. Phir book band karke unhe dobara explain karo.




Step 5 — PYQs solve karo (10–15 minutes)
Topic ke GATE previous-year questions attempt karo. Har galti ka reason note karo.
